# Bengal Stay Scout
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Rahul and his father, a Kolkata-based social media influencer, seeking paid hotel collaborations and hosted stays.
## Product Purpose
Find appealing, sourced 3/4/5-star hotels and resorts across eligible West Bengal destinations. Prioritize properties featured by multiple independent creators.
## Operating Context
Live Google Places discovery; Gemini 3.8 via existing Vertex/Vercel connection; public Instagram, Facebook and YouTube evidence; draft outreach only.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Exclude Purulia district, Sundarbans, Sikkim and Kolkata city hotels. Include destination weekend resorts on Kolkata outskirts when suitable. Cover every remaining West Bengal district through explicit town searches. Never promise exhaustive results. Star category is distinct from guest rating. Main shortlist requires sourced 3+ category; candidate review stays separate. Recent quality evidence is required or flagged unverified. Count a creator once across platforms, group duplicate posts, disclose sponsorship only with explicit evidence. Preserve existing local notes and allow backup import/export. Background jobs persist progress; no outreach is sent.
## Brand Commitments
User rejected old cramped navy/cobalt interface. Dark luxury, charcoal, restrained bronze, much larger property photos, larger typography. Build directly, user chose code-first.
## Evidence on Hand
Actual live Maps listings, photos and attribution. Prior research output needs independent revalidation. No invented creator names, follower totals, rates or performance claims.
## Product Principles
Evidence before ranking. Useful leads before volume. Explicit uncertainties. Resumable research. Keep ordinary restaurant scout untouched.
